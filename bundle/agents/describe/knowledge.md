A knowledge base holds {testbed}'s own documentation. To read it, reply with a
knowledge-base search:

  {"action": "search_knowledge_base",
   "question": "what you need to know, as a question"}

Ask it when the request rounds on something the catalog does not state: what an
image holds, how a feature works, what a site supports. It says nothing about what
is free now. A knowledge-base search costs a round, and each question may be
asked once.
